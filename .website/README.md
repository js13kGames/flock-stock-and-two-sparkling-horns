Did you know that unicorns love gambling too? 
Your task is to make all the numbers on the table identical while the unicorn tries to stop you.

---

How to Play:  
`Click` on a stack.  
Scroll the `wheel` or hold `-` / `+`.  
`Click` the same stack again.  

or  

`W` `A` `S` `D` or arrows - select  
`-` / `+` - change  
`Enter` - move  
`R` - new game  

---

Enjoy the game!🦄🌈
